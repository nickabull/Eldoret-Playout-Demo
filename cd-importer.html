1 · Rip your CD on Donkey

A browser cannot directly rip audio tracks from a USB CD drive. Use fre:ac (free) or Exact Audio Copy on Windows. Select WAV / PCM, 16-bit, 44,100 Hz, stereo, enable accurate/secure ripping if offered, and name tracks Artist - Title.wav. Then select the resulting files below. Don't convert MP3s to WAV; it cannot restore missing audio quality.
